## Outside of work

I live in Connecticut with my wife and our cats. I've loved video games for as long as I can remember, especially Zelda and Pokémon. I also like reading, biking, and playing guitar, bass, and synthesizers.

Games are what got me into technology in the first place. Game Maker got me started making games around 2003, which led to building sites on GeoCities, then to PHP, then to volunteering at a web host, and eventually to a degree in computer science. Around the time I finished my degree, I got back into making games, first as native Android apps and now as the browser games on this site.
