Stackmo is inspired by [Block Dude](https://azich.org/blockdude/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, as popularized by TI graphing calculators: pick up and stack boxes to climb your way to the exit.

It was built from scratch, with stubbornness and determination, on an early version of the engine that became [VastGameKit]({{ '/tools/vastgamekit/' | relative_url }}).
